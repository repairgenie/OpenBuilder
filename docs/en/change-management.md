# Change Management Guide (English)

## Overview
The Change Management module tracks the financial evolution of a project, from potential changes to approved contract modifications.

## Key Features
1. **Change Events**: Capture potential impacts before they become formal orders.
2. **PCO (Potential Change Order)**: Financial estimates for owner-side or contractor-side changes.
3. **PCCO (Prime Contract Change Order)**: Formally approved modifications to the owner contract.
4. **CCO (Commitment Change Order)**: Modifications to subcontractor agreements or purchase orders.
5. **Digital Signatures**: Authorize financial changes with secure, paperless sign-offs.
6. **Budget Reallocation**: Transfer funds between cost codes to manage internal variance.
7. **Financial Forecast**: Real-time projection of final costs including pending items.
8. **AI Predictor**: Identify high-risk cost codes prone to future overruns.

## Workflow
1. Identify a change (e.g., from an RFI or Site Instruction).
2. Create a Change Event and estimate the cost.
3. Generate a PCO and send for owner approval.
4. Upon approval, convert PCO to a PCCO and issue CCOs to relevant subcontractors.
