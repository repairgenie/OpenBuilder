# Quality & Safety (Inspections) Guide (English)

## Overview
The Inspections module ensures project quality and site safety through structured checklists and automated deficiency tracking.

## Key Features
1. **Template Builder**: Create custom inspection forms for specific trades or safety requirements.
2. **Inspection Schedule**: Calendar view of upcoming and recurring compliance activities.
3. **Mobile Execution**: Real-time field data entry with Pass/Fail status for checkpoints.
4. **Photo Evidence**: Attach site photos directly to checklist items for visual proof of compliance.
5. **Auto-Observations**: Automatically trigger a corrective action workflow when an item fails.
6. **Safety Hazards**: High-priority tracking for immediate site dangers (IDLH).
7. **Field Sign-offs**: Formal digital signatures from inspectors to close out reports.
8. **Quality Analytics**: Dashboard insights into compliance rates and common failure points.

## Workflow
1. Select an Inspection Template.
2. Schedule the inspection for a specific date and area.
3. Perform the inspection in the field using a mobile device.
4. Attach evidence for failures and sign off on completion.
5. Track resolution of failed items through the Corrective Actions module.
