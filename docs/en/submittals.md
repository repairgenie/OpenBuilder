# Submittals Guide (English)

## Overview
The Submittals module manages the administrative workflow for material and specification approvals, ensuring that all products used on site meet design requirements.

## Key Features
1. **Automated Numbering**: Maintain a clean, sequential log of all submittal items.
2. **Ball in Court (BIC)**: Real-time tracking of which stakeholder is responsible for the next action.
3. **Review Workflows**: Support for sequential (step-by-step) and parallel (simultaneous) review cycles.
4. **Submittal Packages**: Group related items (e.g., all plumbing fixtures) for efficient processing.
5. **Spec Integration**: Associate submittals with standard MasterFormat spec sections.
6. **Distribution Lists**: Automatically notify the correct team members upon submittal status changes.
7. **Forecasting**: Track lead times and critical approval dates to avoid procurement delays.
8. **AI Log Generation**: Automatically extract required submittals from project specification documents.

## Workflow
1. Create a Submittal Item or Package.
2. Assign the item to the Design Team for review (Ball in Court).
3. Reviewers provide comments and assign a status (Approved, Rejected, Revise & Resubmit).
4. Approved submittals are distributed to field teams for procurement.
