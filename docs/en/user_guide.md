# OpenBuilder User Guide (English)

## Introduction
Welcome to OpenBuilder, the modular platform for professional construction management. This guide covers daily operations and advanced AI features.

## Key Modules

### 1. Dashboard
- **Real-time Metrics**: View project health, budget utilization, and manpower at a glance.
- **Budget Distribution**: Interactive doughnut charts powered by Chart.js.
- **AI Schedule Prediction**: Predict phase completion dates based on current progress.

### 2. RFIs (Requests for Information)
- **Creation**: Submit new queries with attachments and priority levels.
- **Map View**: Visualize the spatial location of RFIs on the project floor plan.
- **Bulk Actions**: Use the floating action bar to export multiple RFIs as PDF or close them in batches.

### 3. Daily Logs
- **Field Reports**: Document daily progress, manpower, and weather.
- **AI Report Generation**: Convert shorthand field notes into professional, polished reports with safety and risk detection.
- **Risk Analysis**: AI-driven detection of safety and scheduling bottlenecks.

### 4. Budget & Financials
- **Cost Codes**: Track original budget, change orders, and committed costs.
- **Scenario Simulator**: Use the "What-If" slider to project the impact of cost increases.
- **Currency Switcher**: View financial data in USD, EUR, or MXN.

## AI Assistant Chatbot
The floating assistant in the bottom-right corner provides project-wide support. You can ask:
- "What is the current budget variance?"
- "Draft a response to RFI #002."
- "Show me the safety risks from yesterday's log."

## Getting Support
Use the integrated help documentation or the AI Assistant for instant guidance.
